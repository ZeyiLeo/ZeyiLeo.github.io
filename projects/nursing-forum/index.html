Healthcare × Communication

Setting the stage for connection

Hosting and on-site support at the Hong Kong International Nursing Forum and an anniversary celebration.

Master of Ceremony and Student Ambassador

My role

Master of Ceremony and Student Ambassador

Focus

Event communication · Healthcare community

The occasion

The Hong Kong International Nursing Forum and HKU School of Nursing 30th Anniversary took place on 30–31 October 2025.

My contribution

As Master of Ceremony and Student Ambassador, I hosted the opening dinner and coordinated with senior alumni to support the event. I also provided on-site support and guest reception services at the forum.

What connects

The work brought healthcare and communication together in a live setting: introducing people, supporting the flow of an event, and welcoming guests.

A formal gathering, and its human flow

This case has two connected settings: the opening dinner and the forum itself. The supplied CV distinguishes the master-of-ceremony role from on-site support and guest reception. Presenting them separately makes the contribution clearer. Hosting concerns the public flow of an occasion; reception and support concern the practical experience around it.

The anniversary context also gives the event a particular audience. Senior alumni are part of the recorded coordination, while the forum brings a healthcare community together. The available group photograph provides a view of that gathering. It should be read as an event record, rather than a measure of how individual guests experienced the occasion.

Hosting is one kind of communication

As master of ceremony for the opening dinner, I worked in a setting where communication is delivered live and forms part of an event’s sequence. Coordination with senior alumni formed part of the recorded responsibilities.

A useful question raised by the role is how a host can give an audience enough orientation to follow an occasion. Names, transitions and the relationship between the next activity and the current one are forms of practical information. This is a reading of what hosting demands, not a reconstruction of an unrecorded speech or backstage exchange.

Support continues away from the microphone

The student-ambassador contribution included guest reception and on-site support. This broadens the account beyond the image of someone speaking on stage. An event also contains arrivals, questions and practical arrangements. The recorded responsibilities place my contribution in that supporting environment.

That distinction connects the forum with the ACT internship. Both include organisation and communication, but they are different experiences with different periods and role definitions. Keeping them separate lets visitors compare what was done in a formal forum setting with the more sustained administrative tasks of an international programme.

The photograph as context

The group image is useful because it makes the collective setting visible. It conveys that this was a gathering involving many people, while leaving individual responsibilities to the accompanying text.

The visual journal places this image beside other public settings, including outreach and literary exchange. A future expansion could add an approved programme or script extract if one becomes available. For now, the clearest account is the documented role, occasion and contribution, supported by the existing event image.
