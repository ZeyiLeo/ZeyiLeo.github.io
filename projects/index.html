Several routes through the work.

The healthcare cases address different settings: a public outreach activity, an international academic programme and a formal forum. The communication cases show expression both on stage and through programme operations. The business case follows an independent venture, while the digital-platform case concerns the site through which these activities are presented.

Filters are a way to find a relevant thread, rather than a claim that the projects are interchangeable. A case may appear under more than one theme because its actual responsibilities cross those boundaries. Within each page, role, context, available materials and further questions remain separately readable.

What a case can tell you.

The contribution sections identify the work supported by the record. The longer notes examine a material or task in more detail, including the difference between an event image and a documented result, or between a launch milestone and continuing operational analysis. Related links let those questions lead to another project, a visual story or a new draft essay.
