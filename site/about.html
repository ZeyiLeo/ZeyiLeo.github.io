A little context

One person.
Several ways of seeing.

I’m Zeyi Li. You can call me Leo. I’m studying at The University of Hong Kong, and I’m drawn to the spaces where different disciplines meet.

Healthcare is a starting point.

My education at HKUMed gives me a place to begin: people, care, and the systems around them. My interests extend into global health, health communication, and the practical questions raised by AI in healthcare.

Communication is the thread.

Bilingual public speaking, hosting, and cross-cultural conversations are part of my experience. I enjoy understanding how an audience sees a subject, then finding a way to make a complex idea clear without losing what matters.

Ideas become useful through practice.

Cross-border e-commerce has brought me into brand building, Amazon operations, and marketing analytics. Programme work at HKU Admissions Office has involved logistics, data, and international student communication. Both ask for attention to the details that help a larger idea work.

There is always another frame.

Cinema, photography, design, and tennis give me different ways to observe, practise, and make things. This site brings those interests into conversation with my academic and professional life.
