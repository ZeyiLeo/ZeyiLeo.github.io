## The project

This website is an ongoing personal project: a place to bring healthcare, communication, business, technology, and creative work into one coherent view.

## The approach

The site uses Jekyll to generate static pages. Shared layouts and structured records keep project information, experience, and the CV maintainable.

The CV page displays the original owner-provided PDF directly, with open and download links to the same unchanged file. Portfolio filters, expandable experience entries, and photography lightboxes support different levels of exploration.

## An ongoing practice

The site will grow through new work, writing, and photographs. It is designed to stay readable, accessible, and useful as those materials evolve.

[View the source on GitHub](https://github.com/ZeyiLeo/ZeyiLeo.github.io).


## A website with several reading speeds

A visitor might arrive with a narrow question about a role, or with an interest in seeing how different activities fit together. The homepage provides a short introduction and selected work. Case pages offer the contribution and context. The timeline preserves dates and role distinctions, while the visual journal lets photographs be read with captions rather than as decoration alone.

These routes have different purposes. A CV download is useful for a formal record. A case can explain an activity in more detail. A new essay can examine a question without pretending to be a past publication. The platform becomes clearer when those forms support each other while retaining their differences.

## Interaction should reveal information

The navigation panel opens a directory of real pages and projects. Its background treatment helps separate the current navigation task from the page beneath it. The two-sided homepage story connects a material or workflow with the background around it. The layered work browser keeps one contribution in focus while showing that other projects remain available.

These three mechanisms serve different actions: finding, understanding a connection and choosing a project. They use normal page scrolling and explicit controls rather than requiring a visitor to learn a custom cursor or a new navigation system. Keyboard alternatives and a complete reduced-motion view remain part of the experience.

## A maintainable factual record

The implementation uses Jekyll layouts and structured records for navigation, experience, selected work and photographs. Reusable templates keep repeated interface elements consistent. The original CV is handled as an immutable document asset, with its hash checked against the supplied file. Website summaries can develop without silently changing the finished PDF.

The original headshot is another deliberate asset decision: the approved JPEG remains unchanged, and the layout respects its portrait proportions. Existing event images are used where they give context to the relevant work. Each project can therefore use the visual form that fits its available material.

## Drafting is different from publication

This iteration adds new essays for local review. They are labelled as drafts and connected to relevant cases. That makes room for editorial development before a text is ready for publication. The same principle applies to explanatory case notes: they can interpret a material and develop questions around it.

A useful next stage is editorial review of what each page helps a visitor understand. More content is worthwhile when it adds a question, material or distinction. The platform is designed to grow through that process, with a recorded baseline and checks for duplicate prose, internal links, responsive layouts and original-file continuity.
