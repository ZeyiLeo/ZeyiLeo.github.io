## The occasion

On **26 April 2025**, I joined the China-Latin American Literature Roundtable Dialogue associated with the Consulate of Colombia and Global Times as an invited roundtable speaker.

## My contribution

I delivered a keynote speech on Sino-Latin American literary exchanges and joined roundtable discussions with the Colombian Ambassador on international relations and cultural cooperation.

## What connects

Speaking across cultural contexts asks for more than fluency. It asks for attention to audience, references, and the different experiences people bring to the same conversation.

This is part of my broader interest in bilingual communication, hosting, and visual storytelling.
