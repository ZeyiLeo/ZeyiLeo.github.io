## The work

During my **June–August 2026 internship with HKU Admissions Office**, I worked on programme operations for HKU Academy for the Talented (ACT).

Responsibilities included participant administration, application processing, and visa tracking across international academic programmes.

## Coordination and clarity

I worked with cross-functional stakeholders on programme logistics and academic events, supporting communication with international students through admissions and onboarding.

The role also involved reconciling admissions datasets, identifying operational discrepancies, and streamlining administrative workflows.

## What connects

This experience links programme delivery, communication, and analytical work. Reliable data and clear communication both contribute to a smoother participant experience.

Student records and internal programme materials are not included in this case study.


## The participant journey has an administrative side

The internship involved HKU Academy for the Talented within the Admissions Office. The documented tasks span applications, participant administration, visa tracking, event logistics and student communication. That range matters because a programme is not encountered only during its scheduled academic activities. It is also encountered through the information and coordination surrounding participation.

An application, an onboarding message and an event arrangement ask different questions of the people handling them. They have to be understood in relation to the programme and to each other. This gives the role a clear operational focus while keeping its boundary intact: supporting programme delivery does not imply responsibility for academic decisions or the design of the entire programme.

## Working with records and discrepancies

Admissions data reconciliation is a specific part of the supplied record. It involves comparing and resolving information rather than simply collecting more of it. The CV also records identifying operational discrepancies and streamlining administrative workflows. These tasks add an analytical dimension to what might otherwise look like an exclusively logistical role.

A useful way to understand this kind of work is to distinguish the record from the action it supports. A status field helps only when the people using it understand what that status means. A discrepancy needs clarification before it can reliably inform a next step.

## Communication across working boundaries

The recorded work included coordination with cross-functional stakeholders and communication with international students through admissions and onboarding. This connects administrative detail with public-facing clarity. Information has to travel between people who do not all need the same level of detail or share the same immediate task.

The contribution is therefore best described through the responsibilities performed: programme administration, logistics, communication and data work. It shows the relationship between the responsibilities without attaching unsupported numbers to them.
