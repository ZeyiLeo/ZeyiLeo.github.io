## The work

During my **June–August 2026 internship with HKU Admissions Office**, I worked on programme operations for HKU Academy for the Talented (ACT).

Responsibilities included participant administration, application processing, and visa tracking across international academic programmes.

## Coordination and clarity

I worked with cross-functional stakeholders on programme logistics and academic events, supporting communication with international students through admissions and onboarding.

The role also involved reconciling admissions datasets, identifying operational discrepancies, and streamlining administrative workflows.

## What connects

This experience links programme delivery, communication, and analytical work. Reliable data and clear communication both contribute to a smoother participant experience.

Student records and internal programme materials are not included in this case study.
