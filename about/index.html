Zeyi Li / Z.Y. Lee / Leo

A background with
several points of view.

HKUMed Bachelor ’29. Healthcare education, practical work and creative interests, considered together.

Here, now.

I’m Zeyi Li, also known as Z.Y. Lee or Leo. I’m studying at The University of Hong Kong’s Li Ka Shing Faculty of Medicine, with expected graduation in 2029. Healthcare is the starting point of my current education. The interests around it include global health, health communication and questions about how AI might be useful in learning and project work.

The work collected here extends beyond a single subject area. It includes an international health-sciences programme, community outreach, admissions-office operations, public speaking and cross-border e-commerce. These are specific experiences with different role boundaries. The five fields in my positioning describe the range of the site; the cases explain what I have actually done within that range.

Before university, a wider set of interests.

Cinema, visual art, literature and traditional theatre were interests before university. So were comparative literature and different languages. My earlier personal website was a place to begin recording parts of that background. Those historical interests are a useful part of this introduction, rather than an earlier professional identity being carried forward unchanged.

There are several questions within that creative background. A film can be approached through its framing and sequence, a literary work through its voice and context, and theatre through the relationship between a text and a public performance. These are directions for personal curiosity.

Photography, design and tennis also remain among the interests recorded for this site. They do not all need a portfolio achievement attached to them. Making room for interests alongside work gives a more complete account of a person than turning every part of a biography into an entry on a professional timeline.

Public expression, in different settings.

The China–Latin American Literature Roundtable in April 2025 gave literary and cross-cultural interests a documented public setting. I participated as an invited speaker, delivered a keynote on literary exchange and joined a roundtable discussion with the Colombian Ambassador. The event is presented as that specific contribution.

At the Hong Kong International Nursing Forum and HKU School of Nursing anniversary, my responsibilities were different: master of ceremony for the opening dinner, coordination with senior alumni, guest reception and on-site support. Comparing the two occasions shows why public expression is more than one activity. An address, a discussion and the flow of a hosted gathering place different demands on the speaker or organiser.

The detail behind an organised experience.

During my June–August 2026 internship with HKU Admissions Office, I supported HKU Academy for the Talented. The work included participant administration, application processing, visa tracking, programme logistics, student communication and admissions data reconciliation. This part of the portfolio is often less visible than a stage or an outreach photograph, but it supplies an important account of actual responsibilities.

My Orbis and U21 Group Leader roles provide two further settings. Orbis involved community outreach planning, with project materials including a booth, printed information and a demonstration. U21 involved research, stakeholder engagement, cross-cultural teamwork and presenting recommendations to an international academic audience. Each connects organisation with communication, while addressing a different audience and purpose.

Business as an operating practice.

Since January 2025, my cross-border e-commerce venture has included brand building, USPTO trademark registration and an Amazon store for North America. Market research, product selection, launch work and continuing analysis form different parts of that account. The case distinguishes those milestones and tasks instead of using a broad founder label to imply every possible business responsibility.

Earlier marketing and shipping assistant work belongs to a separate period and role. It included A+ advertising content, store-data analysis and international shipping coordination. The two roles overlapped in 2025. Keeping their periods and responsibilities distinct makes both support work and independent practice visible.

Questions I’m continuing to explore.

How can health information be easier to approach while keeping its limits clear? What does a cross-cultural audience need in order to follow an idea? How can an operational record help someone choose a useful next action? These questions arise from the kinds of activities already represented here. They are starting points for reflection, rather than claims of completed research.

AI is another area of interest. I am interested in its practical uses and in the questions raised by clinical AI, and hope to examine its uses through further learning. The digital platform itself is a concrete technology project: a maintained collection of pages, assets and interactions that can be inspected and improved.

The new essays develop some of these questions in a different form from a CV or case study. They are clearly labelled drafts for my review. That leaves room to test an argument and refine its language before treating it as a finished public text.
